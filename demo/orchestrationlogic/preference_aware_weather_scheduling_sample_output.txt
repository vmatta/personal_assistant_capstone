WHAT TO HIGHLIGHT LIVE:
1. The system checked saved preferences (long-term memory / RAG) BEFORE checking
   weather at all - Sunday was eliminated on preference grounds alone.
2. The weather check used the ACTUAL forecast for a specific FUTURE date
   (Saturday's date), not just "what's the weather right now" - a new
   get_daily_forecast() capability using Open-Meteo's daily/date-range API.
3. The final recommendation is decisive (not another round of clarifying
   questions), and a natural follow-up completes the booking.
4. Contrast with the edge case: if no location is given, the system asks
   instead of silently assuming a city - an explicit, testable guardrail
   against hallucinated defaults.
